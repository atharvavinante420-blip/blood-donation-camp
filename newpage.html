Blood Donation Guidelines (Eligibility Criteria) :-

  1. Basic Requirements
    • Age: 18 to 65 years
    • Weight: 50 kg or more
    • Good overall health
  2. Before Donation
    • Eat a healthy meal.
    • Drink plenty of water.
    • Carry a valid ID.
  3. After Donation
    • Rest for 10–15 minutes.
    • Drink extra fluids.
    • Avoid heavy exercise.
